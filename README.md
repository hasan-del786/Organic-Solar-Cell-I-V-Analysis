# Organic Solar Cell I-V Analysis

A scientific data analysis project for studying the electrical performance of an Organic Solar Cell using current-voltage (I-V) and power-voltage (P-V) characteristics.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the electrical characteristics of an organic solar cell using voltage and current measurements. It calculates important photovoltaic parameters, generates I-V and P-V curves, and provides an interactive browser-based analysis tool.

The project combines Python-based scientific analysis with a web-based interface to make solar cell performance evaluation easier and more understandable.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze voltage and current measurements of an organic solar cell.
- Generate I-V and P-V characteristic curves.
- Calculate maximum power output.
- Determine short-circuit current (Isc).
- Determine open-circuit voltage (Voc).
- Calculate maximum power voltage (Vmp) and current (Imp).
- Calculate fill factor (FF).
- Estimate power conversion efficiency (PCE).
- Estimate series resistance (Rs) and shunt resistance (Rsh).
- Provide an interactive tool for analyzing solar cell datasets.

## Important Photovoltaic Parameters

### 1. Short-Circuit Current (Isc)
The current generated by the solar cell when the output voltage is zero.

### 2. Open-Circuit Voltage (Voc)
The voltage across the solar cell when the output current is zero.

### 3. Maximum Power Voltage (Vmp)
The voltage at which the solar cell produces its maximum output power.

### 4. Maximum Power Current (Imp)
The current corresponding to the maximum power operating point.

### 5. Maximum Power (Pmax)
The highest electrical power produced by the solar cell.

### 6. Fill Factor (FF)
A parameter used to evaluate the quality of a solar cell's I-V characteristics.

### 7. Power Conversion Efficiency (PCE)
The ratio of electrical output power to incident solar power.

### 8. Series Resistance (Rs)
Represents the effective internal resistance in series with the solar cell.

### 9. Shunt Resistance (Rsh)
Represents the effective parallel leakage resistance of the solar cell.

## Mathematical Formulas

**Electrical Power**

P = V × I

**Fill Factor**

FF = (Vmp × Imp) / (Voc × Isc) × 100

**Short-Circuit Current Density**

Jsc = Isc / Active Area

**Power Conversion Efficiency**

PCE = (Pmax / Incident Light Power) × 100

**Incident Light Power**

Incident Light Power = Light Intensity × Active Area

## Data and Methodology Limitations

The supplied sample dataset is simulated or illustrative and should not be treated as experimentally verified laboratory measurements.

The sample current values contain irregularities, so the calculated photovoltaic parameters are demonstrations of the analysis workflow rather than validated device-performance measurements.

The estimated PCE depends on the assumed active area and light intensity. Series and shunt resistance estimates also depend on the calculation method and available data points.

For scientifically reliable results, use measured I-V data collected under controlled illumination and documented experimental conditions.
