Data with calculated power

Formulas: P = V × I. Isc and Voc come from joining the two points around 0 V and 0 mA (or the closest point if the data does not cross them). FF = Pmax ÷ (Voc × Isc). PCE = Pmax ÷ (intensity × area). Rs and Rsh come from the slope of the last 3 and first 3 points. The Python script uses the same formulas.
